# Human Evaluation Rubric · Juno

## What graders score

- **What you’re scoring:** Juno Signal Synthesizer
- **Value proposition:** Turn product signals from multiple sources into clear, evidence-backed insights that help a Product Manager decide what deserves attention, while keeping the human in control of the final decision

## Dimensions

| Dimension | Question | Scale | 1 or No | 3 | 5 or Yes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Source Signals (deal-breaker) | Is the insight supported by the source signals? | 1 to 5 | The insight makes claims that are unsupported, contradict the source signals, or appear to be invented. | The insight is mostly supported, but some conclusions go beyond what the evidence clearly shows. | The insight is fully grounded in the provided signals and makes a clear connection between the evidence and the conclusion. |
| Important Pattern or Theme | Did Juno identify the most important pattern or theme? | 1 to 5 | Juno misses the main pattern or focuses on something insignificant. | Juno identifies a relevant pattern but misses part of the larger theme or importance. | Juno clearly identifies and summarizes the most important pattern across the signals. |
| Uncertainty  (deal-breaker) | Does Juno communicate uncertainty appropriately? | 1 to 5 | Juno presents assumptions or weak evidence as fact and sounds confident when the evidence does not support that confidence. | Juno recognizes some uncertainty but does not clearly explain what is known versus assumed. | Juno clearly distinguishes evidence from assumptions and calls out missing or conflicting information when appropriate. |
| Insights Useful to PM | Is the insight useful to a Product Manager? | 1 to 5 | The response simply repeats the source information and does not help the PM understand why it matters. | The insight is understandable and somewhat useful but requires additional interpretation by the PM. | The insight is concise, clear, and gives the PM enough context to determine whether the issue deserves further investigation or action. |

## Calibration

- **How many answers you’ll score, and how often:** Review a sample of 10 Juno Signal Synthesizer answers each week.
- **Who scores them with you:** A Product Manager or Product Owner and one other member of the product team familiar with the source signals.
- **What happens when two of you disagree:** The reviewers compare their reasoning against the source signals and rubric definitions. If they still disagree, they discuss what made the scoring ambiguous and update the rubric if needed so future reviewers interpret the criteria consistently.

## Pass bar

An answer passes if it receives an average score of at least 4 out of 5 and passes all deal-breaker questions.

**Fails outright, whatever the average:**

- Source Signals: any score of 1
- Uncertainty : any score of 1
- The answer automatically fails if Juno:  Invents information that is not present in the source signals. Contradicts the available evidence. Presents an assumption as a confirmed fact. Fails either deal-breaker question. Provides a recommendation or conclusion when the available evidence is insufficient without clearly communicating that uncertainty.
